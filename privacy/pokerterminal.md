# Poker Terminal — Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 2026-10-17
**Last updated:** 2026-10-17

## English

Poker Terminal ("the App") is a client for displaying a tournament terminal
page and, where supported, providing native remote control. It is designed
**not to collect personal data**.

### 1. Information We Collect

**We do not collect, transmit, or sell personal information.**

- **No account required.** The App has no sign-up and does not ask for your
  name, email or phone number.
- **No analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs.**
- **No hosting of tournament data.** Tournament content and actions are served
  by the terminal you configure, not by us.

### 2. Data Stored on Your Device

The following is stored **locally on your device only** and is never uploaded
to us:

- The HTTPS terminal address you configure
- App preferences (display, refresh and related settings)
- The built-in default demo address shipped with the App

Uninstalling the App removes this data.

### 3. Network Access

- The App connects **directly** from your device to the terminal address you
  enter. We do not operate a relay or proxy.
- Only HTTPS terminal addresses are supported.
- If the terminal is on a local network, iOS may request **Local Network**
  permission so the App can reach it.
- The App does not scan networks you have not configured.

### 4. Third-Party Terminal Services

The App itself does not collect personal data, but **the terminal service you
configure is a third party**. When the App loads that page, the terminal
operator may receive your IP address, request logs, or other data according to
its own practices. Those data are controlled by that operator under its privacy
policy. We cannot access or control them.

### 5. Permissions

| Permission | Why it is requested |
| --- | --- |
| Network access | To load the HTTPS terminal page and send remote commands |
| Local Network | Only if your terminal is on the same LAN |
| External display | To present the tournament page on a connected display |

All permissions are optional; denying them only disables the related feature.

### 6. Third-Party Services

The App contains no third-party advertising, analytics or crash-reporting SDKs.
If you download the App from the App Store, Apple may collect diagnostic and
usage data under [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).
This is controlled by your system settings, not by us.

### 7. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect
personal information from children.

### 8. Your Rights

Because we do not collect personal data, there is nothing for us to export,
correct or delete on our side. You control the terminal address and settings on
your device and the data held by the terminal operator. For EEA/UK (GDPR) and
California (CCPA/CPRA) users: we do not process or sell personal information as
defined by those laws.

### 9. Data Retention

We retain no data. Local settings persist on your device until you change them
or uninstall the App.

### 10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy. Material changes will be reflected by the
"Last updated" date above and, where appropriate, noted in release notes.

### 11. Contact

Questions about this policy: **your-email@example.com**

## 中文版

**生效日期：** 2026-10-17
**最后更新：** 2026-10-17

Poker Terminal（下称"本应用"）是一个用于显示赛事终端页面并在支持时提供原生遥控的
客户端。我们的设计目标是**不收集个人数据**。

### 1. 我们收集哪些信息

**我们不收集、不上传、不出售任何个人信息。**

- **无需注册账号**，不会索取姓名、邮箱或手机号。
- **无统计、广告或追踪 SDK**。
- **不托管赛事数据**。赛事内容与操作由你配置的终端提供，而非由我们提供。

### 2. 保存在设备本地的数据

以下数据**仅保存在你的设备本地**，不会上传给我们：

- 你配置的 HTTPS 终端地址
- 应用偏好设置（显示、刷新等相关设置）
- 应用内置的默认演示地址

卸载应用即会清除这些数据。

### 3. 网络访问

- 应用从你的设备**直接连接**你填写的终端地址，我们不运营任何中继或代理服务器。
- 仅支持 HTTPS 终端地址。
- 若终端位于局域网，iOS 可能请求**本地网络**权限，以便应用访问该终端。
- 应用不会扫描或上报你未配置的网络。

### 4. 第三方终端服务

本应用本身不收集个人数据，但**你配置的终端服务属于第三方**。当应用加载该页面时，
终端运营方可能依据其自身规则接收你的 IP 地址、请求日志或其他数据。这些数据由该
运营方依据其隐私政策控制，我们无法访问或控制。

### 5. 权限说明

| 权限 | 用途 |
| --- | --- |
| 网络访问 | 加载 HTTPS 终端页面并发送遥控指令 |
| 本地网络 | 仅当终端位于同一局域网时使用 |
| 外接屏 | 在已连接的显示设备上呈现赛事页面 |

所有权限均为可选，拒绝只会关闭对应功能。

### 6. 第三方服务

本应用未集成第三方广告、统计或崩溃上报 SDK。若你从 App Store 下载本应用，
Apple 可能依据 [Apple 隐私政策](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/) 收集诊断与
使用数据。这由你的系统设置控制，与我们无关。

### 7. 儿童隐私

本应用不面向 13 岁以下儿童，我们不会有意收集儿童的个人信息。

### 8. 你的权利

由于我们不收集个人数据，因此我们这边没有可导出、更正或删除的内容。你掌控设备上的
终端地址与设置，以及终端运营方持有的数据。针对 EEA/英国（GDPR）与加州（CCPA/CPRA）
用户：我们不处理、不出售上述法律所定义的个人信息。

### 9. 数据保留

我们不保留任何数据。本地设置会一直保留在你的设备上，直到你修改或卸载应用。

### 10. 政策变更

我们可能更新本政策。重大变更会体现在上方的"最后更新"日期中，并在适当情况下于
版本更新说明中标注。

### 11. 联系方式

如对本政策有疑问，请联系：**your-email@example.com**
